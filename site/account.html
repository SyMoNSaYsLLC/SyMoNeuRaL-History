Billing

Pricing is quoted, not published

SyMoNeuRaL builds custom AI infrastructure, and the cost depends on the hardware, the models and the workload — so there is no price list to put here, and a made-up number would be worse than none. For a real quote, mail symonsayadmin@symoneural.com and describe what you are trying to run.

What this page will never do is show an invented figure. The whole argument of this site is that its numbers are measured; that rule does not stop applying at pricing. If you want the arithmetic behind owning versus renting a card, that is published in full.
